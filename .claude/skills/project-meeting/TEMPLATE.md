## Meeting Note Template

```markdown
---
last_updated: {YYYY-MM-DD}
type: {internal | client | external | milestone}
attendees: [{name1}, {name2}]
tldv_link: {url or empty}
---

# {Meeting Title}

**Date**: {YYYY-MM-DD}
**Attendees**: {Name (Role), Name (Role)}
**Type**: {internal | client | external | milestone}
**Recording**: {tldv link or N/A}
**Previous session**: {link to previous meeting note or N/A}
**Meeting prep**: {link to prep file or N/A}

## TL;DR

{2-3 sentences: what happened, what was decided, what shifted}

## Key Discussion Points

### {Topic 1}

{Attributed viewpoints, direct quotes where impactful}

### {Topic 2}

{Attributed viewpoints}

## Decisions Made

- {Decision with context and rationale}

## Action Items

- [ ] **{Owner}**: {Task} — due {deadline if mentioned} — from {meeting slug}

## Open Questions

- {Unresolved question needing follow-up}

## Sentiment & Tone

{Brief for internal/external; deeper relational analysis for client/milestone}

## Routing Log

{Written after PM confirms routing review}
```

## PM's Goals & Questions
